Analyze Email Dashboard

Email attacks have stopped looking like attacks.

Today's phishing is polite, well written and often sent from a domain that passes every authentication check. EmailGuard AI exists to look past the surface of a message and tell you, in plain language, whether it is safe, and why.

What we are building

Verdicts that explain themselves

Every email is checked by forensic layers and local AI models, then reviewed by an LLM analyst that states its reasons and answers each serious finding.

Evidence you can hand over

Each analysis produces a structured forensic report with indicators, origin trace and methodology, written to be read by an investigator.

Reports that show tampering

The report's SHA-256 hash is anchored on a blockchain, so any later change to the stored report can be detected.

I'm developing the detection pipeline, analysis workflow, forensic reports and web platform with a focus on clarity, evidence and honest communication of uncertainty.
